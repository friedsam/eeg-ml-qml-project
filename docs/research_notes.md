# Research Notes and Blog Learnings

## 🧠 Concepts
- **[LoRA (Low-Rank Adaptation)](https://huggingface.co/blog/peft)**
  - Summary: Efficient fine-tuning of LLMs using low-rank matrices instead of full weight updates.
  - Why it matters: Reduces compute/memory costs, could inspire modular tuning for EEGNet or QML layers.
  - Action: Consider testing LoRA-style modular blocks in later deep learning phase.
